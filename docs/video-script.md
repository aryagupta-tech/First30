# FIRST30 — two-minute video script

Use the complete timed walkthrough in [`final-demo-guide.md`](final-demo-guide.md). It follows the official requirement: minute one demonstrates the citizen journey; minute two explains the product and backend decisions.

The strongest recording path is to download the safe demo kit from the FIRST30 home page and upload its three screenshots manually. This proves that the file picker, local image reading, fact extraction, contradiction detection and source linking are working rather than relying on the one-click prepared case.

Keep the video under two minutes and show, in order:

1. 1930 and bank guidance.
2. Visible demo login.
3. Five-step senior-friendly journey.
4. Three manual fictional screenshot uploads.
5. ₹18,499 versus ₹18,400 conflict.
6. English/Hindi complaint.
7. Clearly labelled demo acknowledgement.
8. Additional bank-statement response.
9. Complete-report and receipt downloads.
10. Downloaded-report change check.
11. Expandable backend processing receipt.
12. Clear statement of what works and what remains mocked.

Never use real personal information or claim that FIRST30 submitted to NCRP, contacted a bank, froze funds or recovered money.
